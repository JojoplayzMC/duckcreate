Server Guidelines & Rules

Keep DuckCreate friendly, fun, and constructive for everyone.

No Griefing

Do not destroy, alter, or deface buildings, structures, or transit lines built by other players.

No Hacking

Unapproved flight, speed hacks, kill-aurea, or malicious client modifications are strictly prohibited.

No Spawn Camping

Do not trap, attack, or continually kill players near spawn points or station hubs.

No Stealing

Respect private claims and storage. Taking items from unsecured containers is strictly forbidden.

Don't Be Annoying

Avoid chat spam, harassment, excessive toxicity, and intentionally disrupting server operations.

All Bugs, Glitches & X-Ray Allowed

Vanilla and modded bugs, game mechanics, duplication glitches, and X-Ray packs are explicitly permitted on this server.
